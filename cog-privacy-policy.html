Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Gideon Paul provides Church of God - Spiritual Songs as a free app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Using the app without an account

You can browse and read the song collections without signing in. The app downloads the song catalogue from Google Cloud Firestore and stores a copy on your device for offline reading. Your reading preferences, welcome choice, favorites, and saved song lists may also be stored on your device.

Account information and saved songs

Sign-in is optional. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, your provider and Google Firebase Authentication process information to verify your identity and maintain your account. This can include your name, email address, provider account identifier, and Firebase user identifier. Google may also provide a profile image URL. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, the email address may be an Apple relay address.

When you use account sync, your favorites and named song lists are stored in Firestore associated with your account identifier so they can be restored and synchronized across your devices. You can sign out in Settings; signing out does not delete your account or its synced data.

Technical information and service providers

Google provides Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, and Apple processes Sign in with Apple requests. These services may process network and technical information, such as IP address, device and app information, and authentication or integrity information, to provide, secure, and diagnose their services. An IP address can indicate an approximate location. See Google's Firebase privacy information, Google's Privacy Policy, and Apple's Privacy Policy.

The current app does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Older versions may have used those services.

Account and data deletion

To delete your account, open Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm, and complete any requested sign-in verification. This deletes your Firebase account and its synced favorites and song lists. If an Apple account is linked and you delete on Android, the app cannot revoke Apple's authorization; you can remove the app from your Apple Account settings. Deleting the app account does not delete your Google or Apple account.

You can also request deletion outside the app by emailing gidzpaul95@gmail.com. Please identify the account you use for Church of God - Spiritual Songs; we may need to verify your identity before acting. Your account and synced data are kept until deleted. Service providers may retain technical data according to their own policies. Data stored locally on a device may remain after account deletion; remove the app's local data or uninstall the app to remove it from that device.

Security

We use safeguards intended to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version on this page. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Gideon Paul at gidzpaul95@gmail.com.
